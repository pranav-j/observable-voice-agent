# Noise clips

The background noise used by audio scenarios is generated in code, not recorded. Every clip comes from `evals/audio/noise.py` and a seed, so the same name always produces exactly the same audio, and no audio files are kept in the repository.

| Clip name | What it is | Source | License |
|---|---|---|---|
| `cafe_<seed>` | Babble from six overlapping speech-band noise "talkers", each swinging in loudness at a syllable-like rate, over a pink-noise floor, with short bursts of dish and cutlery clatter | Generated by `evals/audio/noise.py` | CC0 (our own work) |
| `street_<seed>` | Low brown-noise rumble with slow swells from passing cars, over a pink-noise floor | Generated by `evals/audio/noise.py` | CC0 (our own work) |

Scenarios name a clip and a signal-to-noise ratio, for example `noise: {clip: cafe_01, snr_db: 15}`. Caller speech is normalized to -23 dBFS, and the noise runs underneath the whole call at `snr_db` below that, including the caller's silences.

**Limitation:** this is noise that sounds like a café or a street, not a recording of one. The babble has the spectrum and rhythm of speech but no words, so it's easier on speech recognition than real overheard conversation. Recorded CC0 clips would make the noise scenarios harder and more realistic; any added later should be listed here with their source and license.
